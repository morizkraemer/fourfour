fourfour key detection model: data attribution

The musical key detector ships templates (fourfour-analysis-rs/src/cyankey.rs,
models "clean-fsl", "clean-fsl-fma" and "clean-fsl-fma-seg", the default) that
were fitted on the Freesound Loop Dataset (FSL10K). "clean-fsl-fma" and
"clean-fsl-fma-seg" were also fitted on Free Music Archive clips that carry no
key labels: the FSL10K model labelled them (whole clips, or 10 s segments for
"clean-fsl-fma-seg"), and the templates were refitted on both sets.

Dataset
  Antonio Ramires et al. "The Freesound Loop Dataset and Annotation Tool." Proceedings of the
  21st International Society for Music Information Retrieval Conference
  (ISMIR), 2020. Zenodo record 3967852, licensed CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
