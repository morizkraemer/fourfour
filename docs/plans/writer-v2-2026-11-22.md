# Writer v2: review and plan, 2026-11-22

Review and plan only. No code changed. Sources: our code at `eb236d3` (read, not run), rbxport v1.10.0 (`d0ff75b`, read by a sub-agent, not built), `docs/research/usb-hw-check-2026-11-22.md` (branch `worktree-agent-ab1555596a3351105`), `docs/research/rbx-lift-2026-11-22.md` (other worktree, draft), `docs/research/rbxport-ora-2026-11.md`. "UNVERIFIED" = not checked by running or on hardware. Line numbers are from the commits above.

Decisions taken as given (Merlin, 2026-11-22): fourfour goes GPL (Mo still has to agree), rbxport code may be reused, the writer may be rebuilt, everything stays on branches, and the test is a byte comparison against a Rekordbox export.

## 1. Recommendation

**Mostly (a), narrowed: vendor rbxport's two leaf format crates (`rbl-pdb`, `rbl-anlz`) as forks, put them behind one new stick model, and write our own small orchestrator.** Keep our OneLibrary writer, our waveform meter and `multi_sync`. Do not adopt `rbl-export` or `rbl-onelibrary`.

Why this cut:

- `rbl-pdb` (1.9k lines, 58 tests, depends only on thiserror and tracing) has what ours lacks and what issue #41 needs: a real `export.pdb` **reader**, a page builder with index pages, the 255-row rule, `replace_table`, and a builder reused for `exportExt.pdb`. Ours is a writer with a fixed page map and no reader.
- `rbl-anlz` (3.3k lines, 85 tests, needs one 25-line type from rbl-core) parses any ANLZ file and carries unknown sections verbatim. That is what lets us keep Rekordbox's PSSI, PQT2 and PVB2 for imported and foreign tracks. Ours rebuilds every file from our own model and drops what it does not know.
- `rbl-onelibrary` needs rusqlite 0.40 with vendored OpenSSL; we are on 0.33 and Cargo allows one `libsqlite3-sys`. Our OneLibrary writer works, so we fix it in place (section 2) and avoid the bump.
- `rbl-export` is an 8.7k-line orchestrator with one 1140-line function, built around Rekordbox's share folder and sync records, and pulls LAME, blake3 and rubato. We take its design (stage, verify, publish, recover, manifest) and port `rbl-core/src/durable.rs` piece by piece in a later slice.
- Toolchain matches (both Rust 1.99). thiserror and tracing are already in our `Cargo.lock` through other crates, so no new package enters the build (to confirm in slice 1).

Cost of this choice: we give up the CDJ-3000-proven bytes of our `pdb.rs`. The byte diff (section 4) and one early CDJ check by Mo (slice 3) are the answer to that. The old writer stays the default until v2 passes both. If Mo does not agree to GPL, fall back to (c) with rbxport read as documentation only: same slices, 2 to 3 weeks more.

The lift doc recommends (c). The difference is small in practice: both keep our OneLibrary and waveforms and port the safety layer. This plan differs only on the PDB and the ANLZ container, because #41 forces a PDB reader anyway and writing one from scratch next to a tested one makes little sense.

## 2. Review of our writer

### Structure

- `filesystem.rs` (297 lines) does everything in place: copies audio, writes ANLZ, then rewrites both databases. No staging, no fsync, no verify, no recovery. A pulled stick or a full disk mid-sync leaves audio, ANLZ and databases out of step. Artwork errors are only printed (`eprintln!`).
- The writer model (`models.rs` `Track`) is too thin: no rating, colour, date added, play count, bit depth, composer, My Tags, or stable library id. The writers fill these with constants.
- Translation from the library to stick rows is spread over `pioneer-library` (`prepare_for_usb`, `multi_sync.rs`), `sync.rs` (id remap) and each writer (its own artist/album interning, done three times with slightly different rules).
- Identity on the stick is the audio path (`sync.rs` matches by `usb_path`). A retag that changes the artist folder becomes remove + add with a new id.

| 14 | EXT always has PVB2 and PQT2 built by us, never PSSI. Rekordbox: PSSI on 433 of 1264, PVB2 on 82 | `anlz.rs:218` | hw check |
| 15 | PWV2 limits (max 15, no zero columns) that make Nexus players reject the preview (rbxport #278) | `anlz.rs:201` | UNVERIFIED for ours |

### Missing pieces

`exportExt.pdb` (My Tags), an `export.pdb` reader, PSSI (carry it; nobody authors it, rbxport included), `DEVSETTING.DAT`/`MYSETTING*.DAT`/`djprofile.nxs`, staged write + verify + recovery, compatible copies (FLAC/ALAC to WAV/AIFF/MP3), import back (cues, grids, ratings, history), exact progress and cancel, play history carried from the stick, smart naming for path collisions. `.2EX` exists but has not been on a player.

### Tests

About 20 round-trip tests in `export_roundtrip.rs` read our output with a parser written from the same assumptions as the writer, so a shared misunderstanding passes. There is no fixture from Rekordbox and no byte-level constant. Scale and long-track repro tests are good. `multi_sync` has solid behaviour tests (parallel sticks, failures, owned files). Nothing tests a crash mid-sync.

### What is good and stays

Hardware-proven field knowledge in `reference-code/PIONEER.md`, the ANLZ path hash, the 3-band waveform meter, cue names and colours, the owned-file manifest, free-space planning, multi-stick threads and the event contract.

## 4. Byte-by-byte test

### What Merlin exports (the core reference)

A new, small Rekordbox export. The big stick backup is only a secondary check (table layouts, PSSI statistics, My Tags rows).

**Test set: one playlist `FF-GOLDEN`, 5 tracks, in this order.** Real music, so Rekordbox produces real grids and phrases. Prefer your own or freely licensed tracks: the fixture (titles, waveforms, no audio) goes into the repo.

| # | File | Properties to set in Rekordbox |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | MP3 320 CBR, 44.1 kHz, embedded cover | Hot cues A, B, C (B named "drop" and coloured), 2 memory cues, 1 memory loop (4 beats), rating 4, colour Pink, a comment over 130 ASCII characters, one My Tag |
| 2 | MP3 VBR, same artist and album as #1, no cover | Title with unicode: an umlaut, CJK and Cyrillic characters. No cues |
| 3 | WAV 16-bit 44.1 kHz, no cover, no tags beyond title/artist | Nothing set: the minimal row. Artist = artist of #1 in different letter case |
| 4 | AIFF 24-bit 48 kHz, embedded cover (different image) | Hot cue D as a loop, hot cue H plain (tests the extended list), rating 0, no colour, grid edited by hand once (moved first beat) |
| 5 | FLAC 16-bit 44.1 kHz, album set, artist ends with a dot (`Artist Name.`) | Phrase analysis on (so PSSI appears), 1 memory cue with a comment |

**Rekordbox settings:** note the exact Rekordbox version. Analysis: BPM/grid, key, phrase all on; waveform colour 3Band. Export mode. In Preferences, turn on the option that also writes the new device library (OneLibrary / "Device Library Plus"; exact menu name UNVERIFIED) so `exportLibrary.db` is filled: the old stick had it empty. Leave My Settings export at its default.

**Target:** the SD card INTENSO (FAT32), with **no `PIONEER` and no `Contents` folder** at the start (photos may stay). Never put the card in a player before copying it: players add `RBFLTR.DAT` and history.

**Three exports, each copied off before the next** (Finder copy of the whole card, or just `PIONEER/` + `Contents/`, to `~/Backups/usb-golden/A`, `A2`, `B` on the Mac; a worker pulls `PIONEER/` to dev read-only):

1. **A**: export `FF-GOLDEN` to the blank card. Eject. Copy.
2. **A2**: delete `PIONEER` and `Contents` from the card, export the same playlist again, change nothing. Eject. Copy. (A vs A2 shows which bytes Rekordbox itself changes between two identical exports: that is our normalisation list, measured, not guessed.)
3. **B**: without deleting, in Rekordbox add one more track to the playlist, move one hot cue on #1, export again onto the same card. Eject. Copy. (Shows what an incremental Rekordbox sync does to pages, sequence numbers and deleted rows.)

### What can match, what cannot

| Part | Target | Legitimate differences and how to normalise |
|---|---|---|
| ANLZ `.DAT/.EXT/.2EX`, round trip (read Rekordbox's files into the stick model, write them back) | **Byte-identical files** | None expected. rbxport saw 556 of 562 files equal between two Rekordbox exports; only the databases differed |
| ANLZ authored from our own analysis | Same tag order, header lengths, entry counts, PPTH, path hash | Waveform, grid and key values differ: compare structure only |
| `export.pdb` rows (tracks, artists, albums, genres, labels, keys, colours, playlist tree, entries, artwork, columns, categories, sorts, property) | **Byte-identical row bytes**, matched by id | Property created date (mask). Byte 2 `slot*32` depends on row order (compare after sorting by id, or mask) |
| `export.pdb` pages | Same page header fields, row counts per page, row-group words, free/used sizes, index page bodies, table pointers | Page numbers and sequence numbers: Rekordbox writes incrementally, we write in one pass. Normalise page index to (table, nth page) and sequence to its rank. Whole-file identity for export A is a stretch goal, UNVERIFIED |
| `exportExt.pdb` | Same as above | Same |
| `exportLibrary.db` | Never byte-identical (random SQLCipher salt, page allocation, WAL) | Decrypt, dump schema text + every table as rows sorted by primary key. Mask `property.createdDate`; list other columns that A vs A2 shows as noise |
| Artwork JPEGs | Identical when carried from Rekordbox | Our own resize differs: compare size and count only |
| `playlists3.sync`, `DEVSETTING.DAT`, `djprofile.nxs` | Bytes, when we write them | Timestamp field masked |
| `RBFLTR.DAT`, `USBMNG.DAT`, `-wal`/`-shm`, `._*`, DeviceLibBackup | Ignored | Listed as "not compared" in the dump footer |

### Tool: `stick-dump` and `stick-diff`

Two examples in `pioneer-usb-writer/examples/` (next to `dump_rekordbox_playlist.rs`), built on the v2 stick reader, so the reader is tested by the same act.

- `stick-dump <root> [--raw] [--no-normalise]` prints canonical text, one fact per line, stable order:
  - `pdb header tables=20 pages=41 seq_rank=...`
  - `pdb page table=tracks n=0 rows=5 flags=0x34 w20=5 w22=0 free=... used=...`
  - `pdb row tracks id=3 title="..." artist_id=2 ... ` and with `--raw` `pdb raw tracks id=3 <hex>`
  - `ext row ...`, `onelib schema <table> <sql>`, `onelib row content 3 col=value ...`
  - `anlz /Contents/a/b.mp3 DAT PQTZ len=... hdr=... sha=...` and with `--raw` the section hex
  - `file PIONEER/Artwork/00001/a1.jpg size=... sha=...`
- `stick-diff <a> <b>` dumps both and prints a unified diff; exit code 1 on any difference (use under `set -o pipefail`).
- Normalisation rules live in one function with one test each; every rule cites the A vs A2 evidence.

### Golden fixture in CI (no stick needed)

Fixture: `pioneer-usb-writer/tests/fixtures/rbx-golden/{A,A2,B}/PIONEER/**` without audio (well under 2 MB, UNVERIFIED), plus the committed dumps.

1. **Reader test**: dump of fixture A equals committed `A.dump.txt`.
2. **Noise test**: normalised dumps of A and A2 are equal. Raw dumps differ only on masked lines.
3. **Round-trip test**: fixture A -> stick model -> v2 writer into a tempdir (audio replaced by stub files of the recorded size) -> dump equals `A.dump.txt` at `--raw` level.
4. **Ratchet**: until slice 4 is done the round trip is compared against `expected-diff.txt`. The test fails when the diff changes in either direction, so every slice must shrink the file and commit it. Empty file = byte parity reached.
5. **Our-library test**: export from a `library.db` fixture, verify structure only (tag order, page rules, verification checks).

## 5. Target architecture

```
pioneer-library (library.db)
   | stick_export.rs: one translation, library rows -> StickModel
   v
pioneer-usb-writer/src/stick/
   model.rs     StickModel { tracks, playlists, my_tags, history, settings, foreign }
                StickTrack { export_id, library_id, path, tags, rating, colour, dates,
                             cues, analysis: Carried(sections) | Authored(ours), artwork }
   read.rs      stick root -> StickModel   (export.pdb first, OneLibrary, exportExt, ANLZ)
   plan.rs      old StickModel + wanted -> merged StickModel + file operations (ids kept,
                foreign rows carried, name interning in one place, case rule in one place)
   emit/pdb.rs, emit/ext_pdb.rs      on vendor/rbl-pdb
   emit/anlz.rs                      on vendor/rbl-anlz + our waveform module
   emit/onelibrary.rs                our writer, moved, fed from StickModel
   verify.rs    checks on a written tree (ids unique, files exist, hash dir = PPTH, both DBs agree)
   publish.rs   stage dir on the stick -> verify -> journaled rename -> recover on next run
vendor/rbl-pdb, vendor/rbl-anlz      GPL forks, headers kept, tracing optional
```

`multi_sync.rs` keeps its API and event contract and calls `plan` + `publish` instead of `sync_usb_keeping`. The old `writer/` modules stay until v2 is the default, behind one switch in `multi_sync`.

## 7. Risks

- **GPL not agreed by Mo**: slice 1 falls away; reader and builder get written from the docs (plus 2 to 3 weeks). Decide before slice 1.
- **Vendored code is not hardware-proven** either. Mitigation: slice 3m right after the first write path; old writer stays default.
- **Whole-file PDB identity may be out of reach** (Rekordbox's incremental page history). The plan only depends on row and page-rule identity.
- **Rekordbox version drift**: the fixture pins one version; dbVersion 10000 vs 1000 shows formats move. Record the version in the fixture.
- **Fixture content**: titles, waveforms and cover thumbnails of the test tracks land in the repo. Use own or free tracks, or keep covers out.
- **Staging needs space**: a staged sync holds changed files twice on the stick. Stage only databases and ANLZ, copy audio in place under the journal (UNVERIFIED design, decide in slice 6).
- **Fork upkeep**: 5k vendored lines, upstream ships about 10 releases a month. We take fixes by hand, only when a diff or a deck shows a need.
- **Old players** (NXS, XDJ-1000): nothing here is tested on them. Out of promise until Mo has one.

## 9. Out of scope

- **PRO DJ LINK**: not now. It is a network stack (announce, dbserver, NFS: about 17k lines in rbxport, of which `rbl-prolink`, `rbl-dbserver`, `rbl-nfs` lift cleanly and `rbl-link` does not). It does not fit "lightweight sync, play, organize" yet. Worth a second look once v2 is default, because the dbserver would reuse the same stick model.

- Formatting sticks, Rekordbox `master.db` writes, sync UI changes, smart playlists, history authoring.
